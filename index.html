Look Before You Leap

Confessions and Advice from a Private School Mom

Stay-at-home moms have the most stressful job in the country! Much of that stress comes from handling educational demands and preparing children for college. As a first-time mom, I felt extra pressure since I had to learn everything “on the job.” Being a full-time mom allowed me to put in countless hours—for me, both a labor of love and an internal compulsion to be a great employee. Since I got paid in hugs, smiles, quality time together and the words, “I’ll love you forever, Mom,” I can say with certainty that it was the best job I ever had.

In writing Look Before You Leap, I aimed to do two things. First, I wanted to pass on what I learned over the years as a way of smoothing the path for future first-time moms. Second, since I only had one child, I felt compelled to help others benefit from all my hard work. The book contains 16 confessions, selected high school and college information and analysis, personal stories, and advice from my 13 years as a Private School Mom. To all my readers, I wish you a successful journey through your children’s school years. If you’re thinking of investing in private school services, look before you leap!

“As a connoisseur of admissions, I devoured this book like a mystery novel, the mystery being college admissions. Parents with students in public and especially private school should take the time to read this insightful book to see how admissions is done!”

Michele Hernández Bayliss, author of A Is for Admissions

About the author

M. J. Wilen has a BS in Engineering from The Pennsylvania State University and an MBA from Harvard Business School. She worked as an engineer at Westinghouse, an operations officer at Mellon Bank, a new product development manager at DuPont, a consultant at McKinsey & Company, and as a senior executive at PepsiCo and Yum Brands. She retired from the corporate world in 1999 and worked full-time as a Private School Mom until 2017.

Currently, she enjoys living in sunny Las Vegas, Nevada, where she spends her days researching, writing, working out, and taking advantage of local entertainment. She is an active member of the Society of Children’s Book Writers and Illustrators and a 12X12 Picture Book Challenge Guru.
